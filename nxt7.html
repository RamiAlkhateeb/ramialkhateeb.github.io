NxT7

Everyday apps, with an AI assistant built in.

NxT7 is my product studio. Each app solves one everyday problem, and each has an optional Gemini assistant: plan a week of meals in one sentence, turn a goal into a dated checklist, or find a song for your mood.

Under the hood

Built on one shared system.

Nxt.UI design system

One Blazor component library and set of design tokens powers all three apps, so a fix or a new component lands everywhere at once.

One Gemini client

A shared client asks Gemini for structured JSON, validates every reply, and falls back across models. Each app only writes its own prompt.

Private, installable PWAs

Blazor WebAssembly apps that install on the home screen, work in Arabic and English, and keep the user’s AI key in their own browser.
